# Test · Warm Matte

<!-- Generated from style.json (version 1). Edit the style, not this file. -->

A soft, warm, matte look for testing styles: lower contrast with lifted blacks, recovered highlights, toned-down greens, warm highlights and cool shadows, a light vignette. Auto exposure evens out differently exposed photos (against the group when applied with 'even out'); white balance stays the camera's, 400 K warmer.

## Best for

- cats
- golden hour
- travel
- overcast landscapes

## Avoid on

- night scenes with point lights
- scenes that need deep blacks

## Adaptive rules

- **exposure**: metering middle, use_group True, strength 100.0, max_change 1.5
- **white_balance**: mode as_shot, temperature_offset 400.0, tint_offset 0.0
